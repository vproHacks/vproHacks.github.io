I've been designing and building digital experiences for over 8 years. My work sits at the intersection of bold visual design and functional engineering — where aesthetics serve purpose.

I'm drawn to retro-futurism: the idea that the visual language of the past can feel more alive than the sanitized minimalism of today. CRT monitors, VHS artifacts, neon phosphor — these aren't nostalgia. They're a design system with character.
